# Rights, Privacy & Public Scope

This portfolio describes an **independent unofficial personal fan project**. It was **not** commissioned, approved, endorsed or sponsored by Royal Caribbean.

## What is excluded from this public repo

- Music adapted from the Beatles' *Yellow Submarine*, new lyrics adapted to that composition, backing audio and the recorded vocal performance.
- Royal Caribbean press-center videos, footage extracts, still images, trademark artwork or ship layouts.
- The finished *Legend of the Seas* music video and any unauthorized clips.
- Private Google Drive links, signed media-download links or credentials.
- Passenger names, emails, telephone numbers, booking details, private correspondence and other sensitive information.

## What is included

- Original descriptions of the project's management, QA, architecture and lessons learned.
- A text-based Mermaid workflow diagram and evidence ledger.
- A self-contained FFmpeg mini-demo with **synthetic text and solid-color graphics**, no music or third-party assets.

## Rights and media

A personal or noncommercial fan purpose does **not** automatically authorize adapting a song or reproducing/remixing copyrighted sound recordings or video. Press-center accessibility is not a rights grant for promotional derivatives. Any wider publication, licensing or commercial reuse would require an appropriate rights analysis and permissions.

Using FFmpeg in a commercial product can have license implications depending on the binary build, dependencies, codecs and distribution model. The provided demo is not a declaration that a specific encoder build is cleared for all commercial uses.
